For a game this famous, **Grand Theft Auto VI** has kept its cast remarkably quiet. As launch arrives, **Rockstar still hasn't published a full official voice cast**, and complete-looking lists online should be treated with suspicion. A few names, however, now have real support.

## Lucia Caminos: Manni L. Perez

The strongest confirmation so far comes from The Game Awards. **Manni L. Perez** has been [reported as a Best Performance nominee for GTA 6](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-voice-actors-all-confirmed-and-rumored-cast/), which has been widely taken as confirmation that she plays **Lucia Caminos**.

[Local TV coverage](https://wgntv.com/news/trending/a-couple-of-grand-theft-auto-vi-voice-actors-have-been-revealed-who-are-they/) has also reported on cast members being revealed. Some outlets remain cautious because Rockstar hasn't itself issued credits, so the final word will come from the game. Lucia's performance is one of the things critics are talking about most, and [The Guardian was struck by the emotional weight of the story](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/18/gta-6-reviews-metacritic/).

## Jason Duval: still unconfirmed

As of this writing, no actor has been officially confirmed as **Jason Duval**. Names have been passed around for a long time, but we're not repeating them as fact. Identifying someone from a voice clip or a social media post isn't confirmation, and it isn't fair to the people being named.

## Brian Heder: Stephen Root?

Veteran actor **Stephen Root**, known for *Office Space*, *King of the Hill* and *Barry*, has [reportedly said he's part of the game](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-voice-actors-all-confirmed-and-rumored-cast/), telling the Associated Press in September that he was in the cast. His name has been linked to the role of **Brian Heder**, the Leonida Keys drug runner who rents to Jason. The specific role is best treated as reported until the credits confirm it.

## Others

Actor **Jim Santangeli** has also [been reported as saying he worked on the game](https://gtasix.org/en/guide/cast/). Beyond that, the picture gets murkier. Reports suggest cast members were asked to hold off until November 19, which means a wave of confirmations could arrive over the next few days.

## How we'll know for sure

The credits. Once players reach them, or once Rockstar and the actors are free to speak, the whole cast will come out quickly. Be careful, though: searching for end credits is one of the easiest ways to hit a [major story spoiler](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-avoid-spoilers-launch-week).

## Why Rockstar stays silent

Rockstar has a history of keeping performers low-profile before launch, letting characters exist on their own terms. GTA V's Ned Luke, Shawn Fonteno and Steven Ogg became stars within the fandom only after people had met Michael, Franklin and Trevor. The same could happen here.

## A word about harassment

Fans have spent years hunting for GTA 6's performers. Please don't message people demanding that they confirm a role, and don't share private details. Actors are often under strict agreements, and rumors can attach to people who have nothing to do with the game.
