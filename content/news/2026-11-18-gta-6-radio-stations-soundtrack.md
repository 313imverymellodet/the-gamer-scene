Nothing says Grand Theft Auto quite like the radio, and **Grand Theft Auto VI** is bringing back one of the series' most beloved names. Rockstar has now previewed **six stations**, a brand-new album and an original score, and the full lineup will become clear when players get behind the wheel.

## The six named stations

Fan trackers citing Rockstar's official music previews list the following [stations and hosts](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-radio-stations):

| Station | Sound | Hosts |
|---|---|---|
| **Flash FM** | Pop | Robyn and Alex |
| **Dirty South Classics** | Hip-hop | Trick and Trina |
| **Cocoteo FM** | Latin | El Martillo and La Gata |
| **AfroBank FM** | African music | Burna and Palmsy |
| **Back Country Radio** | Country | MW and Delta Dawne, with Mud Dog Mike |
| **The Chamber 106.6** | Metal | DJ Kerry and DJ Tom |

The return of **Flash FM** is the headline for longtime fans. It was one of the signature stations in 2002's *GTA: Vice City*, so bringing it back for a new trip to the same city is a deliberate piece of nostalgia.

Rockstar has reportedly described the complete selection as running from classic rock to talk radio, but not all of those stations have been named yet. That means more should appear once the game is in players' hands.

## The album and the score

Beyond the stations, there are two separate music projects:

- ***Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*** is a [34-song project made with Atlantic Records](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-radio-stations), built around new material written for the game.
- ***Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score*** comes from French electronic duo **Justice**, with the score album reportedly due **December 11, 2026**.

Justice is a striking choice. The duo's blend of disco, rock and electronic drama fits a neon-soaked Vice City very naturally. The game's music has already earned a [Best Score and Music nomination](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-game-awards-eight-nominations) at The Game Awards.

## What about the trailer songs?

Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road" and The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together" became inseparable from GTA 6's marketing, as we covered in our [guide to the trailer music](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-trailer-music). Don't assume either will be on your car radio. Trailer licensing and in-game soundtrack licensing are separate, and Rockstar's published previews are the place to confirm any individual song.

## How GTA 6 compares with GTA V

GTA V launched with [more than 240 songs across 17 radio stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Grand_Theft_Auto_V), and later updates expanded that substantially. Rockstar hasn't said how large GTA 6's final library will be, so comparisons will have to wait until players can count for themselves.

## What's still unknown

- The total number of stations
- The complete tracklists
- Talk radio details
- Whether a custom "Self Radio" feature returns
- Whether album songs also play on in-game stations
- Whether there's a streamer-safe music option

If you're planning to stream the game, that last one matters. Licensed music has caused copyright headaches for streamers in earlier games, so check the settings menu before you go live.

## A note on reliability

Rockstar's music rollout has spread through official previews, press and fan databases, and some lists online mix confirmed tracks with wishful thinking. Treat any "full tracklist" posted before launch with caution unless it links to Rockstar directly. By the end of launch week, players will have documented everything.

## The bottom line

A returning Flash FM, six very different stations and a score by Justice: the soundtrack looks like one of the game's biggest strengths. All that's left is picking a station and pulling out of the driveway.
