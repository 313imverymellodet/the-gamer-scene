The bigger the game, the bigger the scam ecosystem around it, and nothing is bigger than **Grand Theft Auto VI**. Security researchers have been warning about fake GTA 6 offers for months, and launch week is when scammers push hardest.

Here are the scams you're most likely to see, and how to protect yourself.

## The one rule that covers almost everything

**The only legitimate way to get GTA 6 is to buy it from an official storefront** (PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Store or an established retailer) for PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. Anything outside that is suspect.

## Scam 1: "GTA 6 beta" or "early access"

**There is no public GTA 6 beta and no paid early access.** Rockstar has never announced one. Sites and accounts offering "VIP early access," beta slots or play-it-before-launch builds are scams, usually built to steal payment details or account logins.

## Scam 2: GTA 6 PC downloads and keys

**Rockstar has not announced a PC version.** That means every "GTA 6 PC download," "PC key" or "PC preorder" is fake. At best it's a worthless code. At worst it's malware. Our [PC status page](/gta-6/pc) tracks the real situation.

## Scam 3: Crypto giveaways and QR codes

Expect "Rockstar is giving away GTA 6 to celebrate launch, send crypto to verify" posts, often using deepfaked videos or hacked verified accounts. Rockstar doesn't give away games for crypto. The summer's CyberLeek leaks also promoted a cryptocurrency through QR codes. **Never scan a QR code or connect a wallet** because a GTA post told you to. More background in our [leak coverage](/news/2026-08-20-gta-6-leak-crisis-cyberleek).

## Scam 4: Fake "official" trailers and leaked endings

AI-generated "Trailer 3" videos and "leaked ending" clips are designed to pull clicks toward sketchy sites. A video is only official if it's on **Rockstar's verified channels** or the official GTA VI website.

## Scam 5: Fake money and modded accounts

Once the game is out, expect offers of in-game cash, modded accounts or unlocks. These can get your account banned and often steal your login.

## Scam 6: Phishing emails pretending to be Rockstar

"Your GTA 6 preorder has a problem, log in to fix it" emails are a classic. Don't click links in emails. Go directly to your PlayStation, Xbox or Rockstar account to check.

## How to verify anything GTA 6

1. **Check the source.** Is it Rockstar's verified account or official site?
2. **Check the platform.** PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Anything else is fake.
3. **Check the payment.** Real purchases happen through official stores, never via crypto, gift cards or direct transfers.
4. **Check the urgency.** "Only 50 slots left!" is a pressure tactic.

## If you've already been scammed

- Change your PlayStation, Xbox and Rockstar passwords and enable two-factor authentication
- Contact your bank if you entered card details
- Report the account or site on the platform where you found it

## The bottom line

Buy GTA 6 from an official store for PS5 or Xbox. Ignore betas, PC downloads, crypto giveaways and "leaked" videos. If it sounds too good to be true, it is.
