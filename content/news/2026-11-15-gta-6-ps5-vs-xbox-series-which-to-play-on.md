If you own both consoles, or you're buying one for **Grand Theft Auto VI**, you're probably wondering which version to get. The honest answer four days before launch: **Rockstar has not published performance details for either platform**, so nobody can tell you for certain which runs better.

What we *can* do is lay out what's confirmed and help you decide on the factors that matter.

## What's confirmed for each platform

**PlayStation 5**
- PlayStation's listing labels GTA 6 as **PS5 / PS5 Pro Enhanced**
- **DualSense** vibration and adaptive trigger support
- Offline play, one player

**Xbox Series X|S**
- Launches the same day, November 19, 2026
- Available in both Standard and Ultimate editions

**Not available:** PS4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC have no announced GTA 6 version.

## What "PS5 Pro Enhanced" does and doesn't mean

The PS5 Pro Enhanced label tells you Rockstar has done specific work for Sony's upgraded console. It **does not** promise a particular resolution or frame rate, and Rockstar hasn't said what the Pro version improves. Be wary of videos or posts claiming "confirmed 60fps on PS5 Pro." There's no official source for that. We dig into the frame-rate question in our [60fps explainer](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-60fps-what-we-know).

## How to actually choose

Since raw performance is unknown, decide on the things you *can* know:

**1. Where your friends play.** Rockstar hasn't announced any crossplay plans or a GTA 6 Online launch, but if online arrives later, having the same platform as your crew is the safest bet.

**2. Which controller you prefer.** DualSense haptics are confirmed on PS5. If you like that kind of feedback in driving and shooting, it's a real point in PlayStation's favor.

**3. Xbox Series S vs Series X.** If you're on Xbox, the Series S is the lower-powered machine. Rockstar hasn't published Series S specifics, but it's reasonable to expect the Series X version to look better. Treat that as an expectation, not a confirmed spec.

**4. Storage.** The final install size hasn't been announced. Whichever console you pick, check your free space now. Our [preload guide](/news/2026-11-05-gta-6-preload-guide) covers expansion options.

**5. Which console you already own.** Don't buy new hardware based on rumored performance differences. Wait for launch-day technical analysis.

## What to watch for in launch reviews

When technical breakdowns arrive after November 19, look for three things on your platform: whether the frame rate holds steady during busy city scenes and fast driving, whether there's a choice between quality and performance modes, and how the Xbox Series S compares with the Series X. Those answers matter more than a single headline resolution number.

## Should you wait for PC instead?

There is **no announced PC release date**. Rockstar's past games eventually reached PC (GTA V about 19 months after consoles, Red Dead Redemption 2 about 12 months after), but that's history, not a promise. If playing at launch matters to you, a console is currently the only option. Our [PC status page](/gta-6/pc) tracks every update.

## The bottom line

Play on the console you already own, or the one your friends use. If you're buying hardware specifically for GTA 6 and can wait a few days, launch-week technical breakdowns will answer the performance question far better than any prediction can today.
