> **Updated November 18, 2026:** This guide originally said performance details weren't available. They now are, and we've rewritten our advice accordingly.

**If you have a choice, PS5 Pro is the best console version of Grand Theft Auto VI, PS5 and Xbox Series X are close behind, and Xbox Series S is the one to be cautious about.** All four target 30 frames per second, and none offers a 60fps mode.

## The quick comparison

| Console | Internal resolution | Stability | Our take |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PS5 Pro** | 1440p, PSSR to near-4K | Best | The premium version |
| **PS5** | 1440p | Good, with some dips | A strong default |
| **Xbox Series X** | 1440p, upscaled to 4K | Good, with some drops | Roughly level with PS5 |
| **Xbox Series S** | 720p, FSR 4 to 1080p | Frequent drops reported | Works, with real compromises |

Details come from [Digital Foundry's testing as reported by Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6s-ps5-pro-upgrade-is-bigger-than-you-might-expect-3429387/) and [Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-comparison-shows-difference-between-xbox-series-x-and-series-s). See our [full performance breakdown](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-60fps-what-we-know).

## If you own one console

**Play it there.** Don't buy new hardware just to get a small improvement. PS5 and Series X owners are getting very similar experiences, and a [99 on Metacritic](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99-roundup) suggests nobody's being left out of what makes the game special.

## If you own PS5 and Xbox Series X

It comes down to preference:

- **Choose PS5** if you like DualSense haptics and adaptive triggers, which are confirmed features, or if your friends are on PlayStation.
- **Choose Series X** if your friends, achievements and library are on Xbox. Reports suggest it [can dip slightly lower in big fights](https://www.nacionale.com/en/articles/gta-6-xbox-series-x-vs-ps5-which-runs-better-1440p-30fps-series-s-720p), but the gap appears small. A full comparison from Digital Foundry was still expected.

## Should you buy a PS5 Pro for GTA 6?

Only if image quality and stability matter a lot to you. The Pro's frame pacing is excellent and it adds ray-traced shadows, but it's [still a 30fps experience](/opinion/2026-11-18-ps5-pro-lost-its-best-argument). Don't upgrade expecting a smoother, higher-frame-rate game.

## What about Xbox Series S?

GTA 6 is playable on Series S, and many people will enjoy it. But [reports on Digital Foundry's analysis](https://windowsforum.com/news/gta-6-xbox-series-s-performance-digital-foundry-finds-frequent-sub-30-fps-drops.453047/) describe frequent drops below 30, along with lower resolution and reduced effects. If you're buying a console specifically for this game, it's worth spending more if you can, or at least seeing the Series S version in action first. If it's what you have, turn on VRR if your TV supports it.

## Things that aren't different

- **Launch time:** [midnight local on November 19](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-release-time-every-time-zone)
- **Price:** $79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate in the US
- **Story and world:** the same game
- **Storage:** plan for [up to 212 GB](/news/2026-11-05-gta-6-preload-guide)

## What about online?

Rockstar hasn't announced a GTA 6 Online launch or crossplay plan. If you're choosing with future multiplayer in mind, the safest bet is the same platform as your friends. We track what's known in our [GTA Online explainer](/news/2026-11-15-gta-online-after-gta-6).

## Should you wait for PC?

There's [a report pointing to early 2027](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-pc-early-2027-report), but Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version. If high frame rates matter most and you can dodge spoilers, waiting could pay off. It's a gamble. Follow our [PC status page](/gta-6/pc).

## The bottom line

Own a console already? Use it. Buying fresh? PS5 or Series X is the sensible pick, PS5 Pro is the luxury option, and Series S is the budget choice with the clearest trade-offs.
