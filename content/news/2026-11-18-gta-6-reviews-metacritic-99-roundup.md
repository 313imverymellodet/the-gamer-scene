The verdict is in, and it is about as loud as a verdict can get. **Grand Theft Auto VI** holds a **99 on Metacritic**, tying *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* for the highest score in the site's history. At the time of writing that figure was based on [147 critic reviews](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/18/gta-6-reviews-metacritic/), and [OpenCritic also shows an average of 99](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/gta-6-opens-to-roaring-critical-acclaim-matching-zeldas-historic-score/).

For context, GTA IV scored 98 and GTA V scored 97. Rockstar has now topped both.

## The biggest scores

- **IGN:** 10/10
- **Game Informer:** 10/10
- **GamingBible:** 10/10
- **The Guardian:** 5 stars
- **TechRadar:** 5 stars
- **GamesRadar+:** 5 stars
- **Eurogamer:** 5 stars
- **The Washington Post:** 4 stars
- **Worthplaying:** 9.5/10

## What critics loved

**A world that reacts.** The praise that keeps coming up is not about map size. TechRadar singled out police pursuits that respond to witnesses, cameras and how your character looks. The Washington Post described non-player characters who chase their own goals and respond to you, making even a routine errand unpredictable.

**Everyday life as gameplay.** Game Informer argued that ordinary moments, such as a traffic accident or a suspicious stranger, can be as compelling as the crimes. GamingBible highlighted gyms, beaches and nightclubs as a major draw between missions.

**A more grown-up story.** This may be the biggest surprise. The Guardian was struck by how emotional the game is, and The Verge called it the most mature GTA Rockstar has made. The Washington Post noted that how Jason and Lucia treat each other carries real consequences.

## What critics didn't love

A 99 does not mean nobody had complaints. [The recurring ones](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/18/gta-6-reviews-metacritic/) are specific:

- **Combat and controls.** The Verge found combat clunky and the weapon wheel frustrating.
- **Mission failures.** The Guardian said missions sometimes fail for reasons that aren't clear.
- **Prescriptive missions.** Game Informer felt some missions keep players on a tight leash.
- **30 frames per second.** TechRadar warned the target may disappoint some players. See our [full performance breakdown](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-60fps-what-we-know).
- **A slow start.** Worthplaying said early chapters spend a long time on everyday activities.
- **Uneven satire.** Ars Technica found some of the jokes stale, while Game Informer praised the satire. Critics simply disagree here.
- **Jason vs Lucia.** Ars Technica found Jason less compelling than Lucia.
- **Vehicle realism.** GamingBible found the handling and damage model jarring early on, with repairs that cost money new players may not have.

## Will the 99 hold?

Possibly not. Scores can shift as outlets without early access publish, and [Digital Trends notes](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/gta-6-opens-to-roaring-critical-acclaim-matching-zeldas-historic-score/) that the number could still change. Player scores are the next test, because the general public has barely started. Reviewers also worked with pre-release code, so launch-day patches and busy servers aren't fully reflected.

## Where The Gamer Scene stands

We haven't published a scored review, and we won't assign a number we haven't earned. What we can say is that the critical consensus is historic, and the complaints are narrow enough to be useful: if clunky gunplay or 30fps are dealbreakers for you, that's what to watch for.
