**There is still no official PC version of Grand Theft Auto VI.** But that hasn't stopped people trying to make one, and Take-Two has now responded in a big way.

On **November 24**, Take-Two filed a copyright takedown with GitHub against **Leonida**, an unofficial project trying to get GTA 6 running on PC. According to [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-unofficial-pc-port-project-shut-down-by-take-two/), GitHub processed the notice across **3,007 repositories**: the original project plus more than 2,900 copies, or forks, of it.

## What Take-Two says

According to Beebom, Take-Two's notice says the project infringed its copyrighted works and helped others infringe them. It also cites **Section 2.3 of Rockstar's terms of service**, which bans decompiling, decrypting or reverse engineering Rockstar games.

The project reportedly relied on a **leaked Xbox build** of the game. Players had to supply the files themselves, but Take-Two's position is that the whole effort rests on stolen code. We won't link to or describe where those files came from.

## Did any of it actually work?

Barely. According to [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/384061/vibecoded-gta-6-pc-ports-are-now-rendering-in-game-graphics-despite-take-two-crackdown), some unofficial projects have rendered characters, environments and even some ray-traced lighting. But the performance figures shared online are poor and unverified: from **1 to 3 frames per second** on a high-end graphics card in one report, to around **20 fps** in another, with some setups using **dozens of gigabytes of memory**.

Decrypt says no project has shown the full game running reliably on PC. Beebom also says no fully playable version exists.

## The AI angle

What's new here is how fast these projects moved. Decrypt reports that much of the work came from developers using AI coding assistants, sometimes called "vibe coding." Decompiling a modern console game used to take years of specialist work. AI tools have made it possible for small groups to make rapid, rough progress in days.

That also explains the scale of the takedown. A project that would once have had a handful of copies now spreads to thousands of forks almost immediately. Decrypt reports that some developers have since moved into private groups.

## Why you shouldn't download any "GTA 6 PC" file

This is the part that matters for most readers. With an official PC version still unannounced, the internet is full of links promising GTA 6 on PC. Every one of them is one of these:

- **Malware or a scam.** Fake installers are a classic way to spread viruses, steal passwords and hijack accounts. A "free GTA 6 PC download" is exactly the bait scammers use.
- **Built from stolen code.** Even if it's real, running it means using leaked files Take-Two is actively pursuing.
- **Unplayable.** By every account, the unofficial versions run at slideshow frame rates and break constantly.

Decrypt also reports that the leaker behind the build promoted a **crypto meme coin** linked to the leak. Treat anything that mixes GTA 6 and crypto as a scam.

## So when is the real PC version coming?

Rockstar still hasn't announced a PC release date. GamesBeat has [reported a Q1 2027 target](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-pc-early-2027-report), which Rockstar hasn't confirmed. We track every credible update on our [GTA 6 PC page](/gta-6/pc).

If the history of GTA V is any guide, the official PC version will be worth waiting for. GTA V's PC release came well after consoles and became one of the best ways to play it.

## Why it matters

Take-Two has always been aggressive about protecting GTA, from leaked footage to [fan-made, reverse-engineered versions of GTA III and Vice City](https://pcgamer.com/hackers-release-reverse-engineered-versions-of-gta-iii-and-vice-city-rockstar-busts-them). That isn't changing. What's changing is the speed. AI tools mean unofficial projects can appear and multiply faster than ever, and companies will keep answering with takedowns at this scale.

For players, the advice stays simple: **wait for the official version, and don't download anything that claims to be it.**
