Ever since Microsoft revealed **Project Helix**, the big question has been simple: is the next Xbox a console, or a PC with an Xbox logo?

Microsoft's answer is now on the record. In *Xbox: The 25th Anniversary Magazine*, handed out at Xbox FanFest in Seattle and published in full on Xbox Wire on November 15, **Jason Ronald**, Xbox's vice president of next generation, said Helix is a console that can also play games from your PC. "It is not a PC that can also play console games," he said, according to [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/xbox-project-helix-console-not-pc/). He also called it "the ultimate gaming machine."

## What Microsoft has said

Ronald's comments, as reported by GGRecon, add up to a clear pitch:

- **Console first.** Helix is designed as a console, with PC games as an extra rather than the foundation.
- **Your whole library.** It will carry **four generations** of Xbox games, along with progression across devices.
- **Not about 8K.** Ronald said the next generation isn't defined by 8K resolution or 240Hz refresh rates.
- **Neural rendering.** He described it as an entirely new way to generate a frame, using machine learning to improve image quality more efficiently.
- **Learning from handhelds.** He cited player feedback from the ROG Xbox Ally, the handheld Microsoft made with Asus.

Back in March, at GDC 2026, Microsoft [said it plans to ship alpha hardware to developers starting in 2027](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/microsoft-confirms-its-next-xbox-project-helix-will-go-out-to-studios-next-year-we-plan-to-ship-alpha-versions-of-the-hardware-to-developers-beginning-in-2027).

## What's only been reported

Some outlets have reported a timeline of a handheld in 2027 and Helix itself in 2028. **Xbox hasn't confirmed either.** Treat those dates as reported, not announced.

## What we still don't know

- **Price**
- **Release date**
- **What it looks like**
- **Hardware specifications**
- **Which PC stores it will support**, and how

That last point is probably the most important for players. "Plays PC games" could mean a lot of things, from only games bought on the Microsoft Store to a much more open system. Until Microsoft explains it, nobody can say how much of your PC library would work.

## Why Xbox is making this argument

Microsoft has spent the last few years putting its games on PlayStation, Switch and PC, and pushing Game Pass on almost every screen. That has led many players to ask whether Xbox hardware still has a future.

Calling Helix a **console** is a direct answer. It tells existing Xbox owners that their library, their achievements and their couch setup still matter. Adding **PC games** is the pitch to everyone else: buy this box and you won't be locked into one store.

## Why it matters

Next-generation hardware is still years away, but this week is a useful reminder of the stakes. [GTA 6 runs at 30fps](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-60fps-what-we-know) on current consoles and drops to a 720p internal resolution on the Series S. We've argued that it's [the moment the Series S hit the wall](/opinion/2026-11-18-xbox-series-s-hit-the-wall). The next Xbox will be judged on whether it fixes that kind of compromise without pricing people out.

Ronald's pitch says the right things. The price and the details will decide whether players believe it.
