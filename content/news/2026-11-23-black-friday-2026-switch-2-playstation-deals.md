Black Friday is **Friday, November 27**, and Cyber Monday follows on **November 30**. Nintendo and Sony have both announced their official deals, and the biggest surprise is what isn't on sale.

Here's what each company is offering, based on their announcements as reported. Prices and stock can change quickly, so check the retailer before you buy.

## Nintendo: $50 off the Switch 2 bundle

Nintendo's headline deal is the **Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World bundle for $499.99**, down from $549.99, according to [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/switch-2-mario-kart-world-console-bundle-on-sale-for-black-friday-2026/).

- **Dates:** November 22 to December 5, 2026, in the US
- **What's included:** a Switch 2, Mario Kart World, and three months of Nintendo Switch Online (an individual membership, not a family plan)
- **Some outlets say it's while supplies last**, so don't assume it will be available until December 5

Nintendo is also taking **$20 off** several first-party games, including *Donkey Kong Bananza*, *Kirby Air Riders*, *Metroid Prime 4: Beyond*, *Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2* and *Super Mario Party Jamboree*. Digital discounts run **November 19 to December 2**, and physical copies are discounted at retailers starting November 22.

**Is it a good deal?** If you were already planning to buy a Switch 2, yes. Prices went up after the console's original $449.99 launch, which Siliconera attributes to the RAM shortage and tariffs. At $499.99, you're paying $50 more than that original console-only price and getting Mario Kart World and three months of online on top.

## PlayStation: accessories and VR, not consoles

Sony's Black Friday sale is live, but [according to Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/playstation-black-friday-sale-is-live-now-knocks-up-to-usd100-off-ps5-accessories-psvr2), **neither the PS5 nor the PS5 Pro is discounted this year**. Last year's $100-off PS5 bundle has no equivalent.

What is on sale at PlayStation Direct and participating retailers:

| Item | Discount |
|---|---|
| PS VR2 | $100 off |
| 27" PlayStation gaming monitor | $50 off |
| Pulse Elite headset | $40 off |
| DualSense Edge | $30 off, until December 14 |
| Pulse Explore earbuds | $30 off |
| Access controller | $30 off |
| DualSense controller | $20 off, until December 7 |

Most discounts run until **November 30**. Sony is also discounting physical copies of games including *Marvel's Wolverine*, *Saros* and *Ghost of Yōtei*, plus PlayStation Plus memberships.

**Why no console deal?** Sony hasn't explained it. But with GTA 6 launching the week before Black Friday, Sony may simply not need a discount to sell consoles this month.

## Xbox

*Updated November 28.* Microsoft's Black Friday sale runs from **November 19 to December 2**, according to [PlayDay](https://playday.one/2026/11/17/xbox-black-friday-sale-2026-announced-with-discounts-of-up-to-80/). It reportedly covers more than **2,000 Xbox and PC games** at up to **80% off**, including *Forza Horizon 6* Premium and *Gears of War: E-Day* Premium at 30% off, and *NBA 2K27* at 55% off.

Like Sony, Microsoft doesn't appear to be discounting its consoles. The reported hardware deals are smaller: up to 15% off custom controllers through **Xbox Design Lab**, and in the US, a controller plus two months of Game Pass Ultimate for new subscribers with eligible Samsung TVs. Check the Microsoft Store for exact prices in your region.

## The GTA 6 angle

If you're shopping because of GTA 6, two tips:

1. **Storage may be the best buy of the weekend.** GTA 6 needs [up to 212 GB](/news/2026-11-05-gta-6-preload-guide). A compatible M.2 SSD with a heatsink for PS5, or an official expansion card for Xbox, will help with every big game, not just this one.
2. **Read the box.** The boxed GTA 6 [contains a download code, not a disc](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-no-disc-code-in-box-explained), and some buyers have reported [missing or used codes](/news/2026-11-23-gta-6-launch-problems-psn-box-codes-fixes). Buy from a major retailer and keep your receipt.
