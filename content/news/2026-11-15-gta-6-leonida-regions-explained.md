**Grand Theft Auto VI** doesn't just return to Vice City. It expands outward into an entire fictional state: **Leonida**, Rockstar's take on Florida. Rockstar has officially named six regions so far, and each one points to a different kind of GTA experience.

Here's what's confirmed about each, and an important warning about the "full maps" circulating online.

## 1. Vice City

The heart of the game and the franchise's return to its Miami-inspired roots. Vice City is the major urban center of Leonida: nightlife, beaches, money and everything that comes with it. Fans have waited since 2002's GTA: Vice City (and 2006's Vice City Stories) to return, and this is the modern, fully realized version.

## 2. The Leonida Keys

A chain of island communities, waterways, fishing culture and smuggling routes. This is where **Jason Duval** has worked for drug runners and where he lives on a property owned by Brian Heder. Expect boats, coastal life and the quieter, sketchier side of Leonida.

## 3. Grassrivers

Leonida's wetland territory, inspired by southern Florida's marsh and alligator country. If Vice City is the postcard, Grassrivers is the swamp behind it: airboats, wildlife and isolation.

## 4. Port Gellhorn

A weathered coastal city and former tourism hub. Rockstar's description suggests a place whose best days are behind it, the kind of faded setting where GTA stories often get their grit.

## 5. Ambrosia

An industrial area tied to sugar production and local power. Industry, money and influence tend to mean corruption in Rockstar's worlds, so it's likely to be more than scenery.

## 6. Mount Kalaga National Park

Leonida's northern wilderness: trails, hunting territory and off-road terrain. It's the GTA 6 equivalent of GTA V's Mount Chiliad and Blaine County backcountry, and the clearest sign that Leonida is designed for more than city driving.

## How big is the map?

**Rockstar has not released an official full map or an official size comparison** with GTA V. You'll see confident claims that Leonida is "twice" or "two and a half times" the size of Los Santos and Blaine County. Those are fan estimates, not confirmed figures.

## A warning about fan maps and leaked maps

Two kinds of "GTA 6 maps" are circulating:

1. **Community reconstructions** are impressive fan projects built from trailer frames and screenshots. They're useful, but they're not Rockstar's map.
2. **Allegedly leaked maps** tied to the CyberLeek leaks this summer. Rockstar has not authenticated them, and they may come from an old development build. Our [leak coverage](/news/2026-08-20-gta-6-leak-crisis-cyberleek) explains why.

If you want to discover Leonida for yourself, avoid both until launch.

## Which region are we most excited for?

Vice City is the headline, but Mount Kalaga and Grassrivers are the most interesting additions. Rockstar's best open-world moments, like RDR2's wilderness and GTA V's countryside, happen away from the city. A Florida swamp and a national park give GTA 6 room to surprise people.
