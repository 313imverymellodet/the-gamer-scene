Rockstar picks trailer music the way other studios pick release dates: carefully, and with a message. **Grand Theft Auto VI's** two main trailers have each been built around a single classic track, and both songs found a new generation of listeners almost overnight.

Here's every official GTA 6 trailer song, and what it does and doesn't tell us about the game.

## Trailer 1: "Love Is a Long Road," Tom Petty

The first GTA 6 trailer, released in December 2023, was set to **Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road,"** from his 1989 album *Full Moon Fever*. The trailer broke YouTube's 24-hour view record for a game trailer, and the song's streams spiked right along with it.

The choice was smart. It's a song about restless love and running from bad luck. Paired with Lucia's introduction and the first look at Vice City, it signaled that GTA 6 would be a story about two people, not just a crime spree.

## Trailer 2: "Hot Together," The Pointer Sisters

**Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2** used **The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together,"** an '80s synth-pop track that fit Vice City's neon-and-sunshine identity. Where Trailer 1 felt wistful, Trailer 2 leaned into glamour and momentum, matching a deeper look at Jason, Lucia and Leonida.

Rockstar described Trailer 2 as a mix of gameplay and cutscenes captured in-game on PlayStation 5.

## The official video list

Rockstar's official GTA VI media library lists **11 videos**: Trailer 1, Trailer 2, an official cover-art animation and eight character clips. You can find them on [our GTA 6 hub](/gta-6), with links to the official uploads.

## Are these songs on the in-game radio?

This is the most common question, and the honest answer is **not necessarily**. Trailer music is licensed for marketing, which isn't the same as a radio soundtrack license. Rockstar hasn't published GTA 6's radio station tracklists, so treat "Love Is a Long Road is on the radio" claims as unconfirmed until players can tune in themselves.

GTA's radio has always been one of the series' signatures, from Flash FM in Vice City to Non-Stop-Pop in GTA V. We'll know Leonida's lineup soon.

## The streaming effect

Both songs are a reminder of how much reach a GTA trailer has. Older catalog tracks rarely get a second life on charts and playlists, but a Rockstar placement puts a song in front of an audience in the hundreds of millions overnight. Expect the same thing to happen to whatever Rockstar uses next, and to plenty of tracks on GTA 6's in-game radio once players start sharing clips of their favorite drives.

## What about "Trailer 3"?

Be careful with any video calling itself "GTA 6 Trailer 3" unless it comes from Rockstar's verified channels. AI-generated fakes and spoiler bait are common right now. Our [scam guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-scams-how-to-spot) explains how to verify.

## Why Rockstar's music choices matter

Rockstar treats each GTA reveal like a film trailer, and a single well-chosen song can set the emotional tone of a 100-hour game before anyone touches a controller. Petty's road-weary romance and the Pointer Sisters' '80s heat bracket what GTA 6 seems to be about: two people, one sun-soaked, dangerous state, and a lot of momentum.
