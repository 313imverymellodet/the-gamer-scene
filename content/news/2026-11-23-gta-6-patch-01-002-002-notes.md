**Grand Theft Auto VI** has its first patch since launch, and this time Rockstar has published the notes. Update **01.002.002 on PS5** and **1.0.2.2 on Xbox Series X|S** began rolling out on November 23, according to [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-update-01-002-002-patch-notes/), which links to Rockstar's support page.

That's a change from the run-up to launch. Rockstar pushed two earlier updates, 01.002.000 on November 13 and 01.002.001 on November 16, without public patch notes, [according to dlcompare](https://www.dlcompare.com/gaming-news/gta-6-day-one-patch-two-silent-updates-and-a-psn-outage).

## What the patch fixes

Rockstar's notes list these fixes:

- **Several crashes**
- **Weather** not changing for long periods
- **Autosave** not working correctly
- **Phone calls not triggering**, including calls that move the story forward and calls that unlock missions
- Players being **unable to lose their Wanted Level**
- Players being unable to use the **deadlift bar at the gym**
- Partners not handing over **bags**, which stopped players selling them to fences

The phone call fix is the important one. If your story stalled because a character never called, this update is aimed at you. Some players had reported an early mission they couldn't progress past. Rockstar's notes don't name a specific mission, so if you're stuck, install the patch, load your save and see whether the call arrives.

The autosave fix matters too. Several launch-week reports described players losing progress after a crash. Until you've confirmed the update is installed, save manually before long missions.

## How big is it?

Small, by GTA 6 standards. Dataminer Tex2 put the download at about **604 MB on PS5** and **1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S**, as reported by Vice. That's a relief for anyone who just cleared more than 200 GB for the game.

## Why you might not have it yet

On PS5, Vice reports a staged rollout: about a third of players first, two-thirds roughly three hours later, and everyone about six hours after that. If your console says you're up to date, you may simply be in a later group.

To check or force it:

1. **PS5:** highlight GTA 6 on the home screen, press Options and choose **Check for Update**.
2. **Xbox:** open **My games & apps**, then **Manage**, then **Updates**.
3. Leave the console in **rest mode** with automatic downloads on, and it should arrive on its own.

Don't delete and reinstall the game to get the patch. That means downloading the full game again for no benefit.

## What's still missing

The patch is about stability, not performance. There's still **no 60fps mode** on any console. GTA 6 continues to target 30fps on PS5, PS5 Pro, Series X and Series S, as we covered in our [performance breakdown](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-60fps-what-we-know). Rockstar hasn't said whether a performance mode is planned.

A social media post claiming Rockstar had scheduled another fix for November 25 also did the rounds last week. Nothing on Rockstar's official channels supported it, so treat it as a rumor.

## Why it matters

A first patch that fixes stalled story calls and broken autosaves is the kind of update a game this size almost always needs. The good news is that the list is short and specific. Rockstar's servers largely held up at launch, and most of the noisiest problems were on the PlayStation Network side, which we've covered in our [launch problems guide](/news/2026-11-23-gta-6-launch-problems-psn-box-codes-fixes).
