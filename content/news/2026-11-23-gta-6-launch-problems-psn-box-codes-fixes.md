**Grand Theft Auto VI's** launch went better than many feared. Rockstar's own servers largely held up. But a game sold to tens of millions of people at once was always going to hit problems, and launch week produced three big ones: **PlayStation Network outages, faulty codes in boxed copies, and crashes.**

Here's what's known and what to do.

## 1. PlayStation Network outages

The loudest problem wasn't Rockstar's. Around the launch, PlayStation players reported failed sign-ins, connection errors and trouble downloading games and updates.

[The Guardian reported widespread PSN problems](https://www.dlcompare.com/gaming-news/gta-6-day-one-patch-two-silent-updates-and-a-psn-outage) on the evening of November 18, before the regional unlocks, and Cybernews counted close to **10,000 US problem reports** at one point, according to dlcompare. Xbox players didn't report comparable trouble. Sony hasn't blamed GTA 6 for the disruptions, and reports suggest they were temporary.

**If PSN is the problem:**

- Check the official **PlayStation Network service status** page before you change anything.
- Make sure your PS5 system software is current. dlcompare reports that a system update released November 18 was required before the game would start.
- Restart the console fully, not just from rest mode.
- **Don't delete the game.** Reinstalling won't fix a network outage, and you'll be downloading up to 212 GB again.

## 2. Boxed copies with missing or used codes

This is the most frustrating launch problem, because the GTA 6 box [doesn't contain a disc](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-no-disc-code-in-box-explained). The code is the game.

Before and during launch week, some buyers reported opening boxes that had **no code at all**, or a code that had **already been redeemed**. [Dexerto reported](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/gta-6-players-furious-as-physical-copies-reportedly-arrive-with-missing-or-already-used-codes-3428334/), citing Twisted Voxel, that complaints involved copies bought from GameStop, Best Buy and Rockstar's own store. One widely shared social media post claimed a retailer refused a replacement. That claim hasn't been verified, and Rockstar hadn't confirmed a widespread problem at the time of Dexerto's report.

**If your box has a bad code:**

1. **Go back to the retailer first.** PlayStation's support page for GTA 6 codes advises customers with code problems to contact the place they bought it, including about refunds.
2. **Take photos** of the box, the insert and your receipt before you do anything else.
3. **Keep the receipt and packaging.** The code is single-use, so the retailer will need proof.
4. **Check your account region.** PlayStation codes are region-locked to your account's region, so a code from another country won't work on your account.

A theory spread that thieves were reading codes through sealed packaging. There's no evidence it's happening, and Kotaku's editor-in-chief reportedly couldn't replicate it.

**If you're buying a box as a gift**, buy from a major retailer, keep the receipt and ask the recipient to redeem it straight away.

## 3. Crashes, stalled missions and lost progress

The third set of problems was inside the game: crashes, saves not working correctly, and story phone calls that never came, leaving some players unable to start the next mission.

Rockstar's [first post-launch patch](/news/2026-11-23-gta-6-patch-01-002-002-notes), update 01.002.002 on PS5 and 1.0.2.2 on Xbox, targets exactly these issues. It fixes several crashes, the autosave bug, missing phone calls and a bug that stopped players losing their Wanted Level.

**If your game is crashing or stuck:**

- Install the latest update before doing anything else.
- Load your save again and see whether the stalled phone call arrives.
- Save manually before long missions until you're sure the patch is installed.
- If it keeps happening, report it through **Rockstar Support** with your platform and what you were doing.

## Avoid "fixes" that make things worse

Some advice circulating online can cost you more than the bug:

- **Don't use the "New Zealand trick"** or change your account region to work around problems. It [reportedly breaks Microsoft's terms](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-new-zealand-trick-risk) and could put your account at risk.
- **Don't download "fix" files, mods or "GTA 6 PC" builds.** There's no PC version, and anything claiming otherwise is a [scam](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-scams-how-to-spot).
- **Don't hand your account login to anyone** offering to "unlock" the game or fix a code.
