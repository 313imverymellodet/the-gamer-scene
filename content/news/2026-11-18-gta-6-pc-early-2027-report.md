With **Grand Theft Auto VI** launching on consoles, PC players are asking the same question they've been asking for years: when is it our turn? The official answer hasn't changed. **Rockstar has not announced a PC version or date.** But one report has given PC fans something concrete to argue about.

## The report

In October, [GamesBeat reported](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-releasing-on-pc-much-earlier-than-expected-report-3418569/), according to coverage from Dexerto and others, that Rockstar is targeting the **first quarter of 2027** for a PC release, citing four sources. Rockstar declined to comment.

If accurate, that would be a dramatically shorter wait than for Rockstar's previous big releases.

| Game | Console release | PC release | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTA V | Sep 17, 2013 | Apr 14, 2015 | About 19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | Oct 26, 2018 | Nov 5, 2019 | About 12 months |
| GTA 6 (reported target) | Nov 19, 2026 | Q1 2027? | 2 to 4 months |

## Other claims are floating around

A separate claim from a leaker [points specifically to February 2027](https://www.gtaboom.com/gamesbeat-reports-gta-6-will-hit-pc-in-early-2027), citing former employees. Treat that as unverified. Meanwhile, some observers still think late 2027 is more realistic given Rockstar's history.

Note the difference in quality. A named outlet citing multiple sources deserves more attention than an anonymous post. But neither is an announcement, and both could be wrong, or could describe a plan that later moves.

## What Take-Two has said

Take-Two CEO **Strauss Zelnick** has been careful. At a shareholder meeting, he reportedly [described PC as increasingly important](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-on-pc-gets-a-hint-from-take-two-ceo-at-shareholder-meeting-f894) to the business without confirming a GTA 6 port. He has also said Rockstar traditionally [goes to its core console audience first](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-skipping-pc-at-launch-not-due-to-playstation-deal-but-because-rockstar-focuses-on-the-core-consumer-take-two-ceo-says/), and that such news [would come from Rockstar](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114580/grand-theft-auto-6-pc-and-physical-disc-news-will-come-from-rockstar-confirms-take-two/index.html).

When CBS asked about a PC version this week, [Rockstar didn't respond](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6-release-date-price-what-to-know/).

## Reasons to believe it

- Rockstar's recent games have all eventually come to PC
- PC is a growing priority for publishers
- A shorter gap would capture demand while interest is at its peak
- The game's 30fps console target has made a higher-frame-rate version especially attractive

## Reasons for caution

- A two-to-four month gap would be far shorter than Rockstar's pattern
- Rockstar has not listed any PC product, storefront, price or specs
- The console launch comes first, and post-launch support could change priorities
- Internal targets slip, as GTA 6 itself has shown

## Warning: "GTA 6 PC" downloads are scams

There's a real danger in all this speculation. With no PC version available, **any site offering a GTA 6 PC download, key, crack or beta is fake**, and many are vehicles for malware. Don't install anything claiming otherwise. Our [scam guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-scams-how-to-spot) explains the warning signs, and [cloud services or remote play from your own console](/gta-6/pc) are different things from a PC version.

## Should PC players buy a console or wait?

With reviews this strong and [spoilers coming fast](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-avoid-spoilers-launch-week), the temptation is obvious. If you can wait, an early-2027 window would make holding out much easier. But that depends on a report, not a promise. Don't make a purchase decision based on a date that Rockstar hasn't given.

We keep the official position, the history and every rumor verdict on our [GTA 6 PC status page](/gta-6/pc).

## The bottom line

The Q1 2027 report is credible enough to take seriously and not solid enough to plan around. Until Rockstar says something, the official PC release date remains: not announced.
