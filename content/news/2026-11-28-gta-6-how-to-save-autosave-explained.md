If you've lost progress in **Grand Theft Auto VI**, you're not alone. The game's autosave is less frequent than many players expect, and some players have reported losing hours of play.

Here's how saving actually works, based on testing by [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/be-careful-with-auto-saving-in-gta-6-it-doesnt-happen-as-often-as-you-think-2000758487) and [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-psa-heres-how-gta-6-autosave-works/), and how to protect yourself.

## When GTA 6 autosaves

GTA 6 autosaves **after main story missions and larger side missions**. That's it.

It does **not** reliably autosave after:

- Small side activities and errands
- Free-roam driving, shopping or exploring
- Time spent at gyms, beaches or clubs

Kotaku gives a good example. Buying rum for a neighbor didn't trigger an autosave in its testing. Stealing a car from the impound as part of the main story did. VGC calls the behavior "inconsistent."

**GTA 6 never autosaves during a mission.** Kotaku found it doesn't save mid-mission, "period."

## When you can't save at all

You can't save manually while:

- **In a mission**
- **In combat**
- **Being chased** by police or enemies
- **Moving fast or falling**, such as jumping off a bridge

VGC says Rockstar blocks mid-mission saves so players can't save just before a story choice, check one outcome, then reload and try the other. Whatever the reason, it means you need to finish or cancel a mission before you can save.

## How to save manually

1. Make sure you're **not in a mission, not in combat and not being chased**.
2. Stand still somewhere safe.
3. Press **Options** on PS5 or the **Menu** button on Xbox to open the pause menu.
4. Go to the **Game** section.
5. Choose **Save**, then pick a slot.

## How many save slots are there?

According to VGC, GTA 6 has **15 save slots**, and **one is used by your autosave**. That leaves you 14 manual slots.

Use them. **Don't keep saving over the same slot.** Rotate between at least two or three, so if one save is corrupted or saved at a bad moment, you can go back to another.

## Why you might have lost progress

There are a few common causes:

- **Turning the console off mid-session.** If you quit after an hour of free roam, the last autosave may be well behind you. Kotaku cites IGN's report of a streamer who lost 11 hours after turning off a PS5.
- **Crashes.** Launch week brought crash reports on both consoles. Anything you did after the last save is gone.
- **A launch bug.** Rockstar's [first post-launch patch](/news/2026-11-23-gta-6-patch-01-002-002-notes) fixed a bug that stopped the game **autosaving correctly**. If you haven't installed update 01.002.002 on PS5 or 1.0.2.2 on Xbox, do it now.

Kotaku also highlighted a player who reported losing about $10,000 in stashed in-game money after a manual save. It's unclear whether that's a bug, and Rockstar hasn't commented.

## The safe-saving routine

If you only remember one thing from this guide, make it this:

1. **Save manually before every story mission**, especially heists or missions where you make a big choice.
2. **Save manually after every big free-roam session** before you stop playing.
3. **Rotate slots** so you always have a backup.
4. **Never turn the console off** without saving first.
5. **Keep the game updated.**

## Can you replay missions?

Be careful here too. According to KeenGamer, GTA 6 has **no mission replay**, and some side missions disappear permanently once their chapter ends. If you care about doing everything, keep a manual save from before each new chapter.

## Why it matters

GTA 6 is a [roughly 50-hour story](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-how-long-to-beat) before side content, and many players will put in hundreds of hours. Losing even one long session hurts. Rockstar's patch should help with the autosave bug, but the bigger fix is a habit: save manually, and save often.
