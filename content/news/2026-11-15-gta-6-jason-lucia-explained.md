Every Grand Theft Auto lives or dies on its leads. Niko Bellic carried GTA IV's immigrant tragedy. Michael, Franklin and Trevor gave GTA V its three-way chaos. **Grand Theft Auto VI** goes a different direction: two protagonists, **Jason Duval** and **Lucia Caminos**, whose story is built around their partnership.

Here's everything Rockstar has officially revealed about the pair, and what we still don't know as the game launches.

## The premise

Rockstar's official synopsis is short: Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. When an easy score goes wrong, they're pulled into a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of **Leonida**, and they have to rely on each other more than ever.

That last line is the key. GTA V's three protagonists shared missions but rarely shared feelings. GTA 6 puts a relationship at the center of the story.

## Jason Duval

According to Rockstar's official character material, Jason:

- Grew up around grifters and crooks
- Spent time in the Army
- Later worked for drug runners in the **Leonida Keys**

He's someone who has drifted between structure and crime his whole life. Rockstar describes his relationship with Lucia as central to the story. Jason also lives on a property owned by **Brian Heder**, an old-school drug runner in the Keys who lets Jason stay in exchange for work.

## Lucia Caminos

Lucia's official background is just as specific:

- She learned to fight from her father
- She has spent time in prison
- She's focused on changing the odds for herself and her family
- Rockstar's material says she's originally from **Liberty City**

That Liberty City detail is a nice nod for series veterans. GTA IV's New York stand-in still exists in this universe, and Lucia carries that history into Vice City.

## The people around them

The confirmed supporting cast gives a sense of the world Jason and Lucia move through:

- **Cal Hampton**, an associate more comfortable monitoring Coast Guard radio and conspiracy theories than being in the middle of the action
- **Boobie Ike**, a Vice City figure with interests in real estate, a strip club and music
- **Dre'Quan Priest**, a hustler trying to make it in music, who signs the duo **Real Dimez** through Only Raw Records
- **Raul Bautista**, an experienced bank robber recruiting talent willing to take big risks
- **Brian Heder**, Jason's landlord in the Keys

## What we don't know

Rockstar has deliberately held back a lot:

- **The full voice cast.** Rockstar hasn't published one, though Manni L. Perez has been [linked to Lucia through a Game Awards nomination](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-voice-cast-confirmed-so-far). Jason's actor remains unconfirmed.
- **How far the relationship can bend.** Rockstar's footage has shown switching between the two leads, and [reviews say your choices affect how they treat each other](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99-roundup).
- **Where the story goes.** The "easy score gone wrong" is the hook. Everything after it is unknown, and we'd suggest keeping it that way. Our [spoiler guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-avoid-spoilers-launch-week) can help.

## Why this pairing matters

A crime story about two people who need each other is a natural fit for Vice City's sun-bleached, glamour-and-grime setting, and it gives Rockstar something GTA has rarely had: emotional stakes between its own playable characters. If the partnership lands, Jason and Lucia could become the most memorable leads in the series.
