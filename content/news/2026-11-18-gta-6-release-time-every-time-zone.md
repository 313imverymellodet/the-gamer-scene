**Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, 2026.** That means there is no single global launch moment. The game [rolls out by time zone](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-release-times-when-can-you-start-playing), beginning in New Zealand and moving west as each region reaches midnight.

It has already begun. [Players in New Zealand and Australia are in](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/nz-and-aussie-gamers-among-first-to-play-gta-vi), and everyone else is counting down.

## A wrinkle for US players

Forbes reported that [some Americans may be able to start the night before](https://www.forbes.com/sites/brianmazique/2026/11/12/gta-6-unlocks-at-midnight-on-november-19-but-millions-of-americans-can-start-playing-the-night-before/), and some guides suggest certain US store listings could open when midnight reaches the East Coast. Rockstar's stated rule is local midnight, so treat that as your baseline and check your own console's library. If yours opens early, enjoy it. If it doesn't, nothing is broken.

## Your final checklist

1. **Finish the preload.** Preloading [opened on November 10](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-preload-start-time-download-size/). Your console must have the full game downloaded before it can start at midnight.
2. **Have up to 212 GB available.** That is Rockstar's stated requirement. Guides recommend extra headroom on top. Our [preload and file size guide](/news/2026-11-05-gta-6-preload-guide) has the details.
3. **Install the update before midnight.** Rockstar [told players to download the launch update](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/breaking-news/article/is-gta-6-out-how-people-are-playing-the-new-game-already-plus-what-the-verdict-is-so-far-152323498.html) ahead of November 19 so they are ready to play.
4. **Leave the console in rest mode** with automatic downloads on, so late patches can come through.
5. **If you bought a box, redeem the code now.** The retail box holds a [download code, not a disc](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-no-disc-code-in-box-explained), and you can't start until the game is on your console.
6. **Lock down your feeds.** With New Zealand and Australia hours ahead, spoilers are already possible. Use our [spoiler survival guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-avoid-spoilers-launch-week).

## Can you get in earlier?

People are trying. Changing an Xbox's region to New Zealand has spread online as a way to play hours ahead, but it [reportedly violates Microsoft's terms](https://www.newsweek.com/gta-6-players-access-game-early-with-new-zealand-timezone-trick-12732608), and some players claim suspensions. We explain why it's a bad trade in our [New Zealand trick explainer](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-new-zealand-trick-risk).

## What if it doesn't unlock right away?

A launch this size will put strain on stores and networks. If the game doesn't start at midnight:

- Check that the download and update both finished
- Restart your console
- Confirm your console's date, time and region are set normally
- Check Rockstar's and your platform's official status pages

## The bottom line

Midnight local. Up to 212 GB. Update installed. After thirteen years, you're down to hours.
