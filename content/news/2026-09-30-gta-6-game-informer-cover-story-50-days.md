As of today, **Grand Theft Auto VI** is **50 days away**. The **November 19, 2026** launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S is holding, and Rockstar has moved from teasing to its final marketing push: an extended gameplay presentation in August, a magazine cover story this week, and a hard date for preloads.

Here is where everything stands.

## Game Informer gets the cover story

Rockstar's biggest press moment since August's Netflix premiere is a **Game Informer cover story**. Issue 382 carries a 14-page feature on the state of Leonida, with **12 exclusive new screenshots** and interviews with Rockstar developers. The cover uses original Rockstar artwork of the two leads, **Jason Duval** and **Lucia Caminos**.

Game Informer revealed the cover on September 25. The digital edition went live for subscribers on **September 29**, with print copies reaching subscribers and newsstands over the following weeks.

Rockstar's own teaser for the feature pointed to three areas it had not discussed publicly before:

- **Weather systems** built to simulate Florida's less postcard-friendly conditions, not just sunshine and neon
- **Wildlife** across land, air and sea throughout Leonida
- **How the studio researched Miami** to build its version of Vice City

That list matters because it points to what Rockstar thinks separates GTA 6 from everything else releasing this year. A lot of fall games are selling bigger maps. Rockstar is selling a *simulated place* — somewhere that behaves like somewhere, even when you are not doing a mission.

A note on sourcing: some fan sites have posted detailed "every new fact" lists from the magazine, including claims about map size and vehicle theft mechanics. We are not repeating those until we can confirm them against the issue itself or official Rockstar material. If a detail has not come from Rockstar or Game Informer directly, treat it as unconfirmed.

## The August Extended Look, in brief

Rockstar's **Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look** premiered on Netflix at 3 p.m. ET on August 27, then went free on YouTube and the official GTA 6 site at 9 p.m. ET. Rockstar said the presentation was captured in-game on PlayStation 5.

The showcase arrived a week after alleged gameplay clips from a group calling itself "CyberLeek" spread online. We covered that in [our report on the leak crisis](/news/2026-08-20-gta-6-leak-crisis-cyberleek). Rockstar's official footage has since replaced the leaks as the reference point for what the game actually looks like.

## Preload opens November 12

GTA 6 **preloads begin November 12**, one week before launch. That gives players seven days to download a game that will almost certainly be one of the largest console installs of the generation.

November 12 is also when **physical boxes hit store shelves** — and that leads to the most controversial detail of the launch.

## The physical edition has no disc

The retail box for GTA 6 contains a **download code, not a disc**. Push Square and GameSpot both reported the code-in-box setup in June, and it has not changed since.

In practice, that means:

- Redeeming a boxed copy requires an internet connection and a full download
- There is no offline install and no partial data on physical media
- Boxed copies arrive on the preload date so code-in-box buyers can be ready on launch day

For collectors, and for anyone who likes to trade in or resell games, this is a real loss. For Rockstar, it is a way to control leaks, limit the used market at launch, and deliver a day-one build without printing a patch-dependent disc.

## Pricing and editions

Nothing has changed since preorders opened. The **Standard Edition is $79.99** and the **Ultimate Edition is $99.99**. As we reported in August, early preorder data from Sensor Tower suggested the vast majority of early buyers chose the Ultimate Edition — an estimate Take-Two has not confirmed. See [our August GTA 6 roundup](/news/2026-08-20-gta-6-news-roundup) for the full breakdown.

PlayStation also used its September 3 State of Play to show **two limited-edition GTA 6 DualSense controllers**. Expect those to sell out long before the game unlocks.

## What about PC?

Still no PC date. Rockstar has not announced one, and history suggests a long gap: GTA 5 reached PC roughly 19 months after its September 2013 console launch. We keep a running status page at [GTA 6 on PC](/gta-6/pc), and the official console schedule lives on our [GTA 6 release date page](/gta-6/release-date).

## The Gamer Scene take

Fifty days out, the story is no longer *whether* GTA 6 ships. It is how much of the industry reorganizes itself around it. [October's crowded release calendar](/news/2026-09-30-october-2026-game-releases) exists because publishers are getting out of Rockstar's way.

The Game Informer feature is a classic Rockstar move: pick one outlet, give them a controlled deep dive, and let everyone else report on the report. Expect a steady cadence of screenshots, character profiles and trailers from here to launch — and expect every detail to be checked against August's leaked footage.
