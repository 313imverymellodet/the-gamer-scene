**GTA Online** has been one of the most successful live games ever made, running since 2013 and outliving console generations. With **Grand Theft Auto VI** launching November 19, millions of players want to know what happens next. Does GTA Online shut down? Does progress carry over? When does GTA 6 get its own online mode?

Here's what's actually known.

## The short answer

**Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 Online launch plan, and it has not announced an end date for GTA Online.** Most of what you'll read beyond that is speculation.

## What's confirmed

- GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S
- PlayStation's listing describes GTA 6 as a **single-player experience**
- No GTA 6 Online name, mode list, launch date or player count has been announced

Note that the single-player description reflects the launch listing. It **isn't** proof Rockstar will never add an online component.

## What's been reported

Reporting earlier this year, based on UK employment proceedings, described an **unannounced online service** in development and a **32-player internal test session**. That supports the idea that online work exists.

What it **doesn't** confirm:

- The final player count (an internal test isn't a shipping spec)
- Whether online launches on November 19
- What the mode is called or how it works

Treat "GTA 6 Online launches day one with 32-player lobbies" as unverified.

## Rockstar's history is a mixed guide

When GTA V launched in September 2013, **GTA Online arrived about two weeks later**, in October 2013. Red Dead Online followed Red Dead Redemption 2 by roughly a month as a beta. That pattern suggests a staggered online launch is possible, but Rockstar hasn't said it will repeat it.

## Will GTA Online shut down?

There's no announcement of that. GTA Online still has an enormous player base and makes a lot of money. A sudden shutdown at GTA 6's launch would be surprising, and Rockstar has given no indication of one. More likely, GTA Online keeps running while Rockstar's attention gradually shifts, but that's our analysis, not a confirmed plan.

## Will progress transfer?

**Nothing has been announced.** No account transfer, carry-over of money or vehicles, or progression link between GTA Online and any GTA 6 online mode has been confirmed. Be skeptical of anyone claiming otherwise.

## The economy grumbling

Players have recently criticized GTA Online's economy, especially the cost and payout balance around the **Kortz Center Heist**. Some see that as Rockstar preparing for the transition. There's no evidence those changes reveal anything about GTA 6's online plans.

## The roleplay question

Rockstar acquired **Cfx.re**, the team behind FiveM roleplay servers, in 2023, which makes roleplay and creator tools strategically important. Whether any of that is part of GTA 6 at launch hasn't been announced.

## The bottom line

Keep playing GTA Online if you enjoy it, and don't assume your progress will move anywhere. We'll update our [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6) as soon as Rockstar says something official about online.
