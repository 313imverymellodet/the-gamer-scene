When The Game Awards revealed its 2026 nominees on Tuesday, one name stood out for an unusual reason. **Grand Theft Auto VI** picked up **eight nominations, including Game of the Year**, two days before anyone outside the review circle could buy it.

That has [set off an argument among fans](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/gta-6-sparked-controversy-over-its-tga-2026-nominations-but-it-still-wont-be-able-to-surpass-one-of-the-best-games-of-the-decade/), even as nobody seriously disputes the game's quality.

## The eight categories

According to LevelUp, GTA 6 is nominated for:

1. Game of the Year
2. Best Game Direction
3. Best Narrative
4. Best Art Direction
5. Best Score and Music
6. Best Audio Design
7. Best Performance
8. Best Action/Adventure Game

Its reported Game of the Year rivals are *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* remake, *007 First Light*, *Phantom Blade Zero*, *Control: Resonant* and *Resident Evil Requiem*. Check the [official Game Awards site](https://thegameawards.com/) for the final list, since some nominee lists circulating online are unreliable.

## Is this allowed?

Yes. The rules were set out before the announcement. [The Game Awards' eligibility cutoff](https://gamingbolt.com/gta-6-is-officially-eligible-for-the-game-awards-2026-barely-making-the-cutoff-date) requires a game to be commercially available by **Friday, November 20, 2026, at 9 p.m. PT**. A November 19 release clears that by a day.

The practical oddity is timing. Nominees were announced November 17. Voting [runs until December 9](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/11/the-game-awards-2026-nominees-to-be-announced-next-week-zelda-and-gta-6-eligible), with the ceremony on **December 10** at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. So jurors could nominate GTA 6 based on review access, but the public will have only about three weeks with a game whose main story alone could take [50 hours or more](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-how-long-to-beat).

## Why fans are annoyed

The complaints fall into a few camps:

- **It feels predetermined.** Some fans think a game hadn't been released, so a nomination rewards hype.
- **Other games lose oxygen.** Smaller titles that launched months ago have to compete with the biggest marketing event in the medium.
- **Voters can't finish it.** A game this long makes a considered public vote difficult.

The other side has a simple response: the game earned a historic [99 on Metacritic](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99-roundup), it meets the published rule, and excluding it would make the awards look absurd. If GTA 6 had missed the cutoff, it would have had to wait for 2027's show.

## Not a record

Eight nominations is a big haul, but it isn't the most ever. [LevelUp notes](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/gta-6-sparked-controversy-over-its-tga-2026-nominations-but-it-still-wont-be-able-to-surpass-one-of-the-best-games-of-the-decade/) that *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* received 12 nominations at the 2025 show and won nine awards. GTA 6 can't match that total even if it sweeps its categories.

## The performance category to watch

The Best Performance nomination has another side effect. It reportedly [puts Manni L. Perez forward for her work as Lucia](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-voice-cast-confirmed-so-far), one of the clearest public signals yet about a cast Rockstar has kept quiet.

## What happens next

GTA 6 enters as the obvious favorite, but a remake of *Ocarina of Time* is hardly a soft opponent. It's a strangely fitting final: the two games now tied for [Metacritic's top score](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99-roundup) are connected in this race too, one through the original and the other through its remake.
