It's launch week. **Grand Theft Auto VI arrives Thursday, November 19, 2026** on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and preloading has been open since November 12. After thirteen years, two delays and a summer of leaks, there are now just four days between Leonida and the rest of us.

Here's everything you need to know for the final stretch.

## Where things stand right now

- **Release date:** Thursday, November 19, 2026
- **Platforms:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only
- **Preload:** Live since November 12, per the Rockstar Store listing
- **Editions:** Standard ($79.99) and Ultimate ($99.99)
- **PC:** No PC version or date has been announced

If you haven't preloaded yet, do it now. Launch-day download queues for a release this size can be brutal, and a preloaded copy means you play the moment it unlocks instead of watching a progress bar. Our [preload guide](/news/2026-11-05-gta-6-preload-guide) walks through it step by step for both consoles.

## The questions still open

Even this close to launch, Rockstar has left several practical questions unanswered:

- **Exact unlock times.** Rockstar has not published a regional launch schedule. Don't trust specific hours circulating on social media until Rockstar posts them.
- **Final install size.** Still unannounced. Clear more space than you think you need.
- **Performance modes.** No official frame-rate or resolution options have been detailed for PS5, PS5 Pro or Xbox Series X|S. We break down what's known in our [60fps explainer](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-60fps-what-we-know).
- **Online.** There is no announced GTA 6 Online launch plan.

We'll update our [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6) as each answer lands.

## Your launch-week checklist

**Today through Wednesday:**

1. Confirm your preorder is on the account you'll play on.
2. Start (or finish) the preload.
3. Free up storage. Then free up a bit more.
4. Mute GTA 6 spoiler keywords. Our [spoiler survival guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-avoid-spoilers-launch-week) has the full list.
5. Ignore anyone offering "early access." It doesn't exist. See our [scam guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-scams-how-to-spot).

**Thursday, November 19:**

Watch your console's library rather than refreshing social media. If the game hasn't unlocked yet, it's not you. Rockstar simply hasn't flipped the switch in your region.

## Still deciding which edition?

The $20 difference between Standard and Ultimate buys the Ultimate Edition Upgrade: extra vehicles, weapons, outfits, properties and activities woven into Jason and Lucia's story. Both editions include the Vintage Vice City Pack for eligible early purchases. We weigh whether it's worth it in our [Standard vs Ultimate breakdown](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-standard-vs-ultimate-worth-it).

## Expect a busy launch day

A release this size can strain servers and storefronts. If your game doesn't appear the moment it unlocks, give your console a few minutes, check for a system update, and restart before assuming anything is wrong. If you bought a physical copy, remember Rockstar's own package is a code in a box rather than a disc, so you'll still need to download the game.

## PC players

The hardest news of launch week belongs to PC players: **there is still no announced GTA 6 PC release date.** Rockstar's history suggests a PC version will come eventually, but no year, storefront or system requirements are official. Our [PC status page](/gta-6/pc) tracks every development.

## The bottom line

Four days. Preload now, clear storage, lock down your feeds and ignore the scammers. Thursday belongs to Vice City.
