How much time should you clear for **Grand Theft Auto VI**? The short answer: more than you think. **Most current estimates put the main story at around 50 to 80 hours**, depending on how you play, and a full platinum run is reported at about **200 hours**.

Here's where those numbers come from.

## The numbers at a glance

| Playstyle | Estimated time | Source and status |
|---|---|---|
| Main missions | About 50 hours | Insider Gaming report |
| Story plus optional narrative goals | Around 80 hours | Attributed to Rockstar's Rob Nelson |
| Story plus substantial side content | 80 to 100+ hours | Guide estimates |
| Platinum / all trophies | Around 200 hours | Insider Gaming report |

None of these are official completion figures for your playthrough, and everybody plays differently.

## Why the estimates don't match

Two widely cited figures look contradictory but may not be.

[Insider Gaming's report](https://www.shanethegamer.com/news/how-long-to-beat-gta-6-story-platinum-trophy/), cited by [NBC News](https://nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/grand-theft-auto-six-release-rockstar-games-what-to-know-rcna609644), puts the missions at about 50 hours, with nearly 100 missions spread across five chapters.

Rockstar co-studio head **Rob Nelson** has [reportedly described about 80 hours](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/time-to-beat/) for the main story including optional goals that affect the narrative.

So the difference may simply be how much optional material you do. Someone mainlining missions and someone following every story thread are playing very different lengths of game.

## What reviewers spent

Reviewers offer a useful reality check, because most had to play through a lot in a limited time:

- **IGN** [played more than 120 hours](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/18/gta-6-reviews-metacritic/) and said it was still turning up new details
- **GamingBible** reported [about 70 hours](https://www.gamingbible.com/reviews/gta-6-review-389961-20261117) for its review
- **Digital Foundry** reported [over 50 hours](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114764/gta-6-struggles-to-hold-30-fps-on-base-ps5-more-often-than-expected/index.html) on PS5 Pro during its technical testing

## How that compares with Rockstar's past games

GTA V's campaign is commonly cited at around 30 to 35 hours for main missions, and Red Dead Redemption 2's at around 50 to 60. If the 50-hour figure holds for missions alone, GTA 6's campaign is well beyond GTA V's and closer to Red Dead's scale.

## Why it may feel even longer

Critics suggest this isn't a game to rush. [Worthplaying said the early chapters](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/18/gta-6-reviews-metacritic/) spend considerable time on everyday activities, building the world and the relationship before picking up. Game Informer and GamingBible both emphasized how much of the appeal lies between missions: gyms, beaches, nightclubs and random street incidents.

That's great if you love Rockstar's slower style. If you want constant action, expect a slower opening than GTA V.

## A realistic schedule

If you play about 10 hours a week, here's roughly what these estimates mean:

- **Main missions only:** about 5 weeks
- **Story with optional threads:** about 8 weeks
- **Platinum:** close to 5 months

That's before you lose an evening to driving around Vice City doing nothing in particular, which, if the reviews are right, you will.

Speedrunners and streamers will reach the credits long before most people. One content creator told [AAP](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/nz-and-aussie-gamers-among-first-to-play-gta-vi) he planned to finish in a single sitting.
