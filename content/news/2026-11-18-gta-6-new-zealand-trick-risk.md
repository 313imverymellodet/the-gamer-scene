Because **Grand Theft Auto VI** unlocks at midnight local time, New Zealand got it first. So, inevitably, players elsewhere have tried pretending to be there.

The so-called **New Zealand trick** has spread across social media today. The idea is simple: change your console's region so it thinks you're in the first time zone to reach November 19, and start playing hours before your local launch. [Reports suggested US Xbox players could get in as early as 6 a.m. ET on Wednesday](https://www.newsweek.com/gta-6-players-access-game-early-with-new-zealand-timezone-trick-12732608), roughly 18 hours ahead of the East Coast's midnight.

We're not going to walk you through it, because we don't think you should do it.

## Does it actually work?

Reports conflict. Newsweek described players saying it worked on Xbox but could not independently verify it. [Vice was also cautious](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-release-date-launch-times-new-zealand-method/), saying the method had not been confirmed when it wrote about it.

On PlayStation, the barrier is much higher. [Push Square's guide](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-release-times-when-can-you-start-playing) says it would mean creating a separate New Zealand account and buying the game through that store. Save data from that account can't move to your main profile. In other words, you'd be paying for a second copy and leaving your progress behind when you go back.

## The real risk

Newsweek reports that the method **violates Microsoft's terms of service**, and that some gamers say their accounts were suspended. We can't verify individual suspension claims, and neither Rockstar, Microsoft nor Sony had commented in that report. But think about what's on the table:

- Your entire digital library
- Any subscriptions tied to the account
- Your GTA 6 purchase itself
- The one thing you've waited thirteen years to play

The upside is a head start measured in hours. The downside, if enforcement happens, is being locked out on launch day.

## Other problems nobody mentions

**Store and payment issues.** Regional changes can affect your wallet balance, subscriptions and which store you see. Some accounts are limited in how often they can change region.

**Spoilers and patches.** Being early doesn't protect you from anything, and you may run into problems before support channels are staffed for your actual region.

**Scam risk.** Any website or video offering a "New Zealand access code," a pre-made account or a paid workaround should be treated as a scam. There's no such thing as an official early-access product. Our [GTA 6 scam guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-scams-how-to-spot) covers how those operations work.

## The legit way to feel early

If you want to be among the first in your area, the boring steps genuinely work:

1. Finish your preload now. It needs [up to 212 GB](/news/2026-11-05-gta-6-preload-guide).
2. Install Rockstar's launch update before midnight.
3. Check [the unlock time for your region](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-release-time-every-time-zone).
4. Keep your console on and connected.

## New Zealand, for its part, is enjoying this

The country's tourism board [played along on Instagram](https://www.newsweek.com/gta-6-players-access-game-early-with-new-zealand-timezone-trick-12732608), acknowledging players who were trying to virtually move there. And local internet providers have their own story: Chorus's network strategy manager [called the 212 GB file unprecedented](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/nz-and-aussie-gamers-among-first-to-play-gta-vi), equal to more than a quarter of a typical household's monthly data.

## The bottom line

A few hours of Vice City isn't worth gambling your account. The game will still be there at midnight, and by every early indication, it's going to be worth the wait.
