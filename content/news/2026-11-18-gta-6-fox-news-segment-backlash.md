It wouldn't be a Grand Theft Auto launch without a cable-news argument about it. This time the debate started on **Fox & Friends**, where host **Rachel Campos-Duffy** said she didn't think **Grand Theft Auto VI** was good for culture and society, and [described protagonist Lucia as a "hooker,"](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/fox-news-grand-theft-auto-vi-backlash_n_6afd0851e4b0e902c23c2c90) according to HuffPost. Her co-hosts quickly corrected her.

The clip spread fast, and the response from players was mostly mockery.

## What's actually known about Lucia

Rockstar's official description of **Lucia Caminos** has nothing to do with that label. She learned to fight from her father, has spent time in prison, and is trying to change the odds for herself and her family. She's one of two playable leads in a story about a criminal partnership. You can read her official background in our [Jason and Lucia explainer](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-jason-lucia-explained).

## How the internet responded

[HuffPost reports](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/fox-news-grand-theft-auto-vi-backlash_n_6afd0851e4b0e902c23c2c90) that social media users accused the show of fearmongering, and some pointed out that Fox had reacted in similar fashion when GTA V launched in 2013. Others noticed the irony that the series has long parodied cable news through its own fictional network, **Weazel News**.

## A very old argument

The criticism isn't new, and neither is the backlash to the criticism. Grand Theft Auto has been a lightning rod for decades:

- **Early 2000s:** GTA III and Vice City drew fierce debate over violence
- **2005:** the "Hot Coffee" scandal around hidden sexual content in San Andreas reached the US Congress and changed the game's rating
- **2013:** GTA V's torture mission and depiction of women drew widespread criticism

One commentator [compared the present moment directly with Hot Coffee](https://www.gtaboom.com/from-hot-coffee-to-gta-6-the-panic-around-grand-theft-auto-never-changes-7ec4), arguing the same panic returns with every release.

## It's not only cable news

GTA 6's content is drawing scrutiny in several places at once. [South Korea's ratings board came close to refusing the game](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-south-korea-rating-nudity) over nudity before granting it an adults-only rating. In Florida, [local officials are arguing](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-miami-vice-city-takeover-backlash) about whether Vice City branding belongs on public landmarks.

At the other end of the debate, a University of Tennessee history professor told [Australian Associated Press](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/nz-and-aussie-gamers-among-first-to-play-gta-vi) that the game may actually be less provocative than earlier entries. Early reviews offer some support for a more complicated picture: [The Guardian was surprised by the game's empathy](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/18/gta-6-reviews-metacritic/), and The Verge called it the series' most mature entry.

## Is there a fair criticism in here?

Yes, and it's worth separating from the mistake. This is an adults-only crime game with violence, drugs, sexual content and strong language, and it's reasonable for parents and commentators to discuss that. Ratings exist for exactly this reason, and no child should be handed this game by accident.

What doesn't help is commentary that hasn't done the basic homework. When a critic gets the lead character wrong, the audience stops listening to the part that might have mattered.
