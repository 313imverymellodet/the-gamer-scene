**Grand Theft Auto VI** nearly hit a wall in one of the world's most important gaming markets, and the reason may surprise you. According to meeting minutes [first obtained by Inven Global and reported by Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/gta-6-south-korea-age-rating-nearly-refused/), South Korea's **Game Content Rating Board (GRAC)** seriously considered refusing the game a classification, which would have blocked its release in the country.

The issue wasn't crime or gunfire. It was nudity.

## How the decision unfolded

The reported timeline runs like this:

- **September 1:** Rockstar submits its rating application.
- **September 10:** The first review splits **3-3** between refusing classification and granting an Adults Only rating. The board postpones its decision and advises changes.
- **September 28:** The board receives a playable build.
- **October:** The next review is pushed back so researchers can examine the game's large volume of content.
- **Mid-October:** The final vote goes **8-1 in favor of an Adults Only rating**.

An Adults Only rating in Korea allows sale to adults. A refusal would have meant no legal release there.

## What concerned the board

Members reportedly focused on how often nudity appears without narrative context. Others on the committee argued that the exposure wasn't especially sexual in nature. [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-has-far-too-much-genital-exposure-according-to-south-korean-regulators/) and [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/gta-6-south-korea-age-rating-almost-refused-classifcation-nudity/) also covered the regulators' concerns.

The final descriptors listed for the game are **Sensuality, Violence, Crime, Drugs and Language**.

## Why the board changed course

Two factors stand out in the reporting:

**International precedent.** The majority weighed the fact that ten other ratings bodies, including the ESRB and PEGI, had already rated the game. No regulator wants to be the lone holdout on the biggest release of the decade without a very strong reason.

**New rules.** GTA 6 was reportedly the first game assessed under standards GRAC relaxed in September 2025, which permit an Adults Only rating for suggestive bodily exposure when there are no graphic sexual acts. One dissenting member warned that this could mean more background nudity in future role-playing games.

Some reporting also connects the case to the summer's leaks, suggesting explicit material seen in CyberLeek clips wasn't found in the review build. That's less firmly established, so we're not treating it as settled.

## A decision with bigger implications

GRAC has a long history of tough rulings, and this outcome matters beyond one game. If the most-watched release in years got through under the relaxed standard, other publishers now have a clearer sense of what the board will accept. Korea is also a major market where a refusal would have been a commercial and cultural headline in its own right.

## What this means for players elsewhere

If you're outside Korea, nothing changes. GTA 6 launches at [midnight local time on November 19](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-release-time-every-time-zone) on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

If you're a parent, take the descriptors seriously. This is an adult game with adult content, and a story about two criminals is only part of why. Console parental controls exist for good reason.
