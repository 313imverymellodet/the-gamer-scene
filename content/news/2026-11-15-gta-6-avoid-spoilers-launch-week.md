**Grand Theft Auto VI** has already been one of the most leaked games in history, and launch week is when spoilers get really dangerous. Once the game unlocks on November 19, millions of players will post clips, screenshots and story reactions at once. If you won't finish the story on day one, you need a plan.

Here's how to protect yourself.

## Why GTA 6 is unusually spoiler-risky

- **Summer leaks.** A source calling itself CyberLeek released footage and an alleged map earlier this year and threatened to leak more, including the ending. Rockstar never authenticated the material, but it's still floating around. Our [leak coverage](/news/2026-08-20-gta-6-leak-crisis-cyberleek) has the background.
- **A huge simultaneous audience.** Fast players will reach major story beats within hours.
- **Thumbnails.** Video titles and thumbnails can spoil a twist without you clicking anything.

## Step 1: Mute keywords everywhere

Add these to your mute lists on X, Threads, Instagram, TikTok and Reddit:

- GTA 6, GTA VI, GTA6
- Jason Duval, Lucia Caminos, Jason, Lucia
- Leonida, Vice City
- Supporting cast names: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista, Brian Heder
- "ending," "final mission," "twist" combined with GTA
- CyberLeek

## Step 2: Platform-specific moves

**X and Threads:** Use muted words and set them to apply to your timeline and notifications. Turn off trending topics for launch week.

**YouTube:** The home feed is the biggest risk. Pause watch history, or avoid the home page and use subscriptions only. Use "Don't recommend channel" on GTA-focused channels for a couple of weeks.

**TikTok:** Long-press and choose "Not interested" on the first GTA clip you see, then use the content filter to block the keywords above.

**Reddit:** Leave or temporarily mute GTA subreddits. Even well-moderated communities can't stop every spoiler title.

**Discord and group chats:** Tell your friends where you are in the story, and ask for a spoiler channel or spoiler tags.

## Step 3: Watch out for "official" videos

Expect fake "official" uploads, such as leaked ending videos and AI-made trailers. Only Rockstar's verified channels are official. Anything else promising exclusive story footage is spoiler bait at best and a scam at worst. See our [scam guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-scams-how-to-spot).

## Step 4: Be careful with guides

Searching "how to beat [mission]" can surface articles whose headlines or images reveal later story content. If you get stuck, search the exact mission name and avoid broad story searches.

## Step 5: Turn off notifications from gaming apps

News apps, gaming sites and even your console's own notifications may push headlines about GTA 6 during launch week. Mute push alerts from gaming news apps for a couple of weeks, and check your console's notification settings so friend activity and trending clips don't pop up mid-session.

## How long should you stay locked down?

A big Rockstar campaign can take dozens of hours. If you can't play for long sessions, keep your filters on for two to three weeks. The spoiler wave usually peaks in the first ten days.

## The bottom line

Mute the keywords, tame your YouTube and TikTok feeds, distrust "official" leaks and give friends fair warning. Thirteen years of waiting deserves a clean first playthrough.
