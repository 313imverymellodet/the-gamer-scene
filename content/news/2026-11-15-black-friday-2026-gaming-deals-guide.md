This year, gaming's two biggest shopping moments land back to back. **Grand Theft Auto VI** launches Thursday, November 19, and **Black Friday** follows on November 27, with **Cyber Monday** on November 30. If you're planning to buy a console, more storage or games for the holidays, a little preparation goes a long way.

This isn't a list of deals. Prices change hourly and we won't make them up. It's a guide to buying smart.

## 1. Know the real price before the sale starts

The oldest Black Friday trick is the inflated "original price." Before November 27, write down the current price of anything you're considering. If the "sale" price is barely lower than what you saw two weeks ago, it isn't much of a sale.

Price-history tools for major retailers can show you whether a discount is real.

## 2. Storage is the smartest GTA 6 purchase

Rockstar hasn't announced GTA 6's install size, but it won't be small. If you're running short:

- **PS5:** a compatible M.2 SSD with a heatsink is the standard upgrade
- **Xbox Series X|S:** official storage expansion cards are the plug-and-play option

Storage is one of the categories that reliably goes on sale over Black Friday weekend, and it's a better long-term buy than most game bundles. Our [preload guide](/news/2026-11-05-gta-6-preload-guide) covers storage prep.

## 3. Be careful with console bundles

Check exactly what's included:

- **Which console model?** Disc vs digital, standard vs Pro, Series X vs Series S.
- **Is the game a code or a disc?** Rockstar's own GTA 6 package is code-in-box with no disc, so a bundled "physical" copy may be a code.
- **Which GTA 6 edition?** Standard or Ultimate is a $20 difference. Our [edition breakdown](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-standard-vs-ultimate-worth-it) explains it.

## 4. GTA 6 deal traps to avoid

GTA 6 will be the most-searched game of Black Friday, and scammers know it:

- **"GTA 6 PC" deals.** There is no announced PC version. Any PC key is fake.
- **Too-cheap key resellers.** Unofficial key sites selling GTA 6 well below retail are a gamble at best.
- **Fake storefront ads** on social media that copy real retailers' branding.
- **"Free GTA 6" giveaways** asking for card details or crypto.

Our [GTA 6 scam guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-scams-how-to-spot) goes deeper.

## 5. Don't forget the subscriptions

PlayStation Plus and Xbox Game Pass sometimes run promotions around Black Friday. Before stacking a discounted subscription, check what you actually play and read the auto-renew terms. The same goes for the promotional **GTA+** month that comes with GTA 6 preorders: it renews unless you cancel.

## 6. Buy from retailers with good return policies

Holiday return windows are often extended. Check them before buying, especially for hardware you might want to swap later.

## 7. Wait for real GTA 6 performance info before buying hardware

If you're choosing between a PS5, PS5 Pro or Xbox mainly for GTA 6, remember Rockstar hasn't published performance modes. Technical analysis will arrive in the days after November 19, which is still before Black Friday. Read our [PS5 vs Xbox guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-ps5-vs-xbox-series-which-to-play-on) first.

## The bottom line

Check real prices now, prioritize storage, read bundle fine print and avoid anything GTA 6-related that sounds too good to be true. The best Black Friday purchase is the one you'd have been happy to make at full price.
