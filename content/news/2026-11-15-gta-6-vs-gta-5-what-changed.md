**Grand Theft Auto V** launched on September 17, 2013 and became one of the best-selling entertainment products ever made. **Grand Theft Auto VI** arrives on November 19, 2026, more than thirteen years later. That's the longest gap between mainline GTA games in the series' history.

So what's actually different? Here's a side-by-side using only confirmed information.

## At a glance

| | GTA V | GTA VI |
|---|---|---|
| Original release | September 17, 2013 | November 19, 2026 |
| Setting | Los Santos and Blaine County (San Andreas) | Vice City and the state of Leonida |
| Real-world inspiration | Southern California | Florida |
| Protagonists | Michael, Franklin, Trevor | Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos |
| Launch platforms | PS3, Xbox 360 | PS5, Xbox Series X|S |
| PC at launch | No (arrived April 2015) | No (no date announced) |
| Standard launch price (US) | $59.99 | $79.99 |

## Protagonists: three criminals vs one partnership

GTA V's big idea was switching among three very different men. **Michael**, a retired bank robber; **Franklin**, a young hustler; and **Trevor**, pure chaos. Their stories intersected, but they were never really a team.

GTA 6 builds its story around **Jason and Lucia**, two people pulled into a conspiracy after an easy score goes wrong and forced to rely on each other. It's the first time a mainline GTA has made a relationship between playable leads the center of the story. Our [Jason and Lucia explainer](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-jason-lucia-explained) covers what Rockstar has revealed.

## Setting: San Andreas vs Leonida

GTA V packed Los Santos and the surrounding Blaine County countryside into one map. GTA 6 brings back **Vice City**, last seen in a mainline game in 2002, and expands it into an entire state with six confirmed regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Our [Leonida map guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-leonida-regions-explained) goes region by region.

**How much bigger is it?** Rockstar hasn't said. "Twice the size of GTA V" claims are fan estimates.

## Platforms and PC

GTA V launched on PS3 and Xbox 360, then was re-released on PS4 and Xbox One and reached PC in April 2015, about 19 months after the original launch. GTA 6 launches on current-generation consoles only, and **no PC date has been announced**. See our [PC status page](/gta-6/pc).

## Price

GTA V's standard edition launched at **$59.99**. GTA 6's Standard Edition is **$79.99**, with a **$99.99** Ultimate Edition. Our [Standard vs Ultimate breakdown](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-standard-vs-ultimate-worth-it) explains the difference.

## Online

GTA V's story mode launched first, and **GTA Online** followed about two weeks later, in October 2013. Rockstar hasn't announced a GTA 6 Online plan. Our explainer on [GTA Online after GTA 6](/news/2026-11-15-gta-online-after-gta-6) covers what's known.

## What we can't compare yet

Gameplay systems, mission structure, wanted levels and map size are things players will be able to compare properly once they've spent real time with the game. Early reviews [suggest big changes, including a more grounded driving and damage model](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99-roundup). One thing is already known: GTA 6 [targets 30fps on every console](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-60fps-what-we-know).

## The bottom line

On paper, GTA 6 trades GTA V's three-way chaos for a two-person crime story, swaps Los Santos for a whole state built around Vice City, and costs $20 more. The real comparison starts Thursday.
