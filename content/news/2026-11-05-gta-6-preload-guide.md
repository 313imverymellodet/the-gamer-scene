> **Corrected November 18, 2026:** An earlier version of this guide said preload would begin November 12 and that the install size hadn't been announced. Preloading began **November 10**, and Rockstar's stated requirement is **up to 212 GB**. We've rewritten the guide with the current information.

**Grand Theft Auto VI preloading is open now, and you'll need up to 212 GB of free space.** The game [unlocks at midnight local time on November 19](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-release-time-every-time-zone), so if you haven't started the download, start it now.

## When preload began

According to [Game Rant's breakdown of Rockstar's announcement](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-preload-start-time-download-size/):

- **PS5:** 12:00 a.m. local time on November 10, rolling by region
- **Xbox Series X|S:** 12:00 a.m. GMT on November 10 worldwide, which was the evening of November 9 in North America

Boxed copies reached retailers on November 12. They contain [a download code rather than a disc](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-no-disc-code-in-box-explained), so you redeem the code on your console's store and preload from there.

## How big is GTA 6?

Rockstar says the download needs **up to 212 GB, depending on your device**. For comparison, Game Rant lists GTA V's enhanced version at about 87 GB on PS5 and 94 GB on Xbox Series X|S. GTA 6 is more than double that.

Two other figures are going around:

- **209.75 GB:** an [early report of the PS5 download](https://timesaver.gg/blog/gta-6-file-size-storage-ps5-xbox) from preload screens
- **225.3 GB:** a [reported Xbox store listing maximum](https://fenixbazaar.com/2026/11/07/gta-6-file-size-pre-load/), which may reflect a different way of counting storage units

Neither replaces Rockstar's figure. Plan around 212 GB, then add headroom. Guides commonly recommend another **15 to 25 GB** for unpacking and patches. In practice, that means about **240 GB free** is a comfortable target.

And that viral 676.7 GB screenshot from earlier this year? It was never accurate.

## Where can you install it?

Game Rant says the game needs **internal storage or a compatible M.2 NVMe SSD** on PS5, and external USB drives aren't supported for playing it. On Xbox, the equivalents are the internal drive and official storage expansion cards.

## PS5 checklist

1. Confirm your purchase is on the account you'll play with.
2. Check Settings → Storage.
3. Delete or move games until you have the space.
4. Start the download from your library or the PlayStation Store.
5. Keep the console in rest mode with internet access on.

## Xbox checklist

1. Confirm the game is in My Games & Apps → Full Library.
2. Check Settings → System → Storage.
3. Make room or add an expansion card.
4. Start the download and stay connected.

## Don't forget the update

Rockstar has [told players to download the launch update](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/breaking-news/article/is-gta-6-out-how-people-are-playing-the-new-game-already-plus-what-the-verdict-is-so-far-152323498.html) before midnight on November 19. If your preload finished days ago, check that nothing is queued.

## How long will it take?

At a steady 100 Mbps, 212 GB takes roughly five hours. At 50 Mbps, you're looking at around 10 hours. At 25 Mbps, closer to 19. Real speeds are often lower, and networks will be busy. New Zealand's Chorus called the file [unprecedented](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/nz-and-aussie-gamers-among-first-to-play-gta-vi), equal to more than a quarter of a typical household's monthly data. If you have a data cap, check it first.

## The bottom line

Free up about 240 GB, finish the preload, install the update and leave your console connected. Then the only thing left is waiting for midnight. Everything else you need is on our [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6).
