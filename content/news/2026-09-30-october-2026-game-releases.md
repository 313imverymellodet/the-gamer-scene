October 2026 is not a normal month. It is the last clean runway before **Grand Theft Auto VI** arrives on **November 19**, and nearly every publisher with a fall game has done the same math: launch in October, get four weeks of attention, and be out of the way before Rockstar takes over the conversation for the rest of the year.

The result is the most crowded month on the 2026 calendar. Xbox has its biggest exclusive of the year and the new Call of Duty. Sony has a returning action-RPG it has been showing for years. Konami brings back Castlevania. Nintendo has a Switch 2 exclusive and a new hardware bundle. Square Enix and Capcom both have major Switch 2 releases. And that is before the smaller games that will get buried.

Here is every date worth circling, in order.

## Week 1: October 1–4

**Ghost of Yōtei Complete Edition — October 1 (PS5).** Announced at the September 3 State of Play alongside a new run-based bounty mode called Most Wanted, which lets players take on hunts as different characters — including a returning Jin Sakai. It is the easiest pickup of the month for anyone who skipped Sucker Punch's game last year.

**Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve — October 2 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC).** Bandai Namco's first mainline Ace Combat since 2019 was playable on the floor at Tokyo Game Show, and early access for some editions is already underway. If you have been waiting for a flight game that treats spectacle and melodrama as features, this is it.

Also on October 1: the review embargo for **Gears of War: E-Day** lifts at 9 a.m. ET, five days before launch. More on that below.

## Week 2: October 5–11

This is the week that will break budgets.

**Gears of War: E-Day — October 6 (Xbox Series X|S, PC, Game Pass day one).** The Coalition's prequel goes back to Emergence Day, the night the Locust surfaced, with a campaign that supports four-player online co-op and two-player split-screen. Early access starts October 2. It is the most important Xbox first-party release of the year, and we break down [everything you need to know before it launches](/news/2026-09-30-gears-of-war-e-day-reviews-early-access).

**Star Wars: Galactic Racer — October 6 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC).** Podracing is back, and it launches the same day as Gears. That is a brutal slot for a game that needs word of mouth.

**Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival — October 8 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, Switch 2).** Saber Interactive's first-person horror game is now confirmed on all four platforms at **$39.99** for the Standard Edition and **$49.99** for Deluxe. We covered [what makes this adaptation promising — and risky](/news/2026-07-17-clive-barker-hellraiser-revival-october-8-release-date) when the date was announced.

**Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III] — October 8 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, PC).** The complete mainline saga to date — HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX, HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue, and Kingdom Hearts III with Re Mind — running natively on Switch 2 for the first time. To be clear, this is *not* Kingdom Hearts 4, which Square Enix has committed to **late 2027**. We got that wrong in June and [have corrected our original report](/news/2026-06-09-kingdom-hearts-4-switch-2-october-8).

**Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen — October 9 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, Switch 2).** Capcom's expanded edition adds the frozen Norgan region and brings the full game to Switch 2.

## Week 3: October 12–18

**Planet Zoo 2 — October 13.** Frontier's management sim sequel gives builders something to do while everyone else is shooting at each other.

**Castlevania: Belmont's Curse — October 15.** Konami's 40th-anniversary return for the series was revealed at February's State of Play and dated at the June Xbox Showcase. A mid-October Castlevania is exactly the right kind of seasonal timing.

## Week 4: October 19–25

**Final Fantasy Resonance — October 22 (Switch, Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC).** Square Enix is launching this one on five platforms at once, including both Switch generations — a sharp contrast to the timed exclusivity that defined the series for most of the last decade.

**Nintendo Switch Sports Resort — October 22 (Switch 2).** Nintendo's only first-party Switch 2 exclusive of the month, with twelve sports including table tennis, archery, skateboarding and a returning prop plane mode. Nintendo is also selling a **$529.99 Switch 2 bundle** with a digital copy and three months of Nintendo Switch Online.

**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 — October 23 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, Switch 2).** Infinity Ward's sequel ran two beta weekends in August and was part of Xbox's Tokyo Game Show broadcast. The Switch 2 version launching the same day as every other platform is the clearest sign yet that Microsoft's Nintendo commitment is real.

## Week 5: October 26–31

**Phantom Blade Zero — October 29 (PS5, PC).** S-Game's kung fu action-RPG follows Soul, an assassin with 66 days left to live, and has been a PlayStation showcase regular since its 2023 reveal. Launching three weeks before GTA 6 is a bold call for a game that asks players to learn a demanding combat system.

## The GTA 6 shadow

Nothing on this list is competing with GTA 6 head-on, and that is the point. Rockstar has set **November 12** as both the digital preload date and the day code-in-box physical copies hit shelves, which means many players will stop buying new games entirely in early November. For everything we know with 50 days to go, read [our GTA 6 status report](/news/2026-09-30-gta-6-game-informer-cover-story-50-days).

## The Gamer Scene take

The October pileup is a direct consequence of GTA 6 finally holding its date. A year ago, publishers were hedging. Now nobody wants November, and the result is five major releases in the week of October 5 alone.

Our advice: pick two. If you have Game Pass, **Gears of War: E-Day** is effectively free. If you want the strongest pure single-player swing, **Phantom Blade Zero** is the one we are most curious about. Everything else can wait for a holiday sale — and some of it will be discounted before GTA 6 even launches.
