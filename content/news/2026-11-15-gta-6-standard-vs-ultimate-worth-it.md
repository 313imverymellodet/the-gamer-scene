**Grand Theft Auto VI** comes in two editions, and if early preorder data is any indication, most fans have already chosen the expensive one. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said early preorders were "skewing more to the Premium Edition," and third-party estimates from Sensor Tower put the Ultimate share near 89% of early orders. Take-Two hasn't confirmed that figure.

But early preorders come from the most dedicated fans. If you're still deciding, here's what each edition actually includes.

## The two editions at a glance

| | Standard | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|
| Price (US) | $79.99 | $99.99 |
| Base game | Yes | Yes |
| Vintage Vice City Pack | Yes (eligible early purchases) | Yes |
| Ultimate Edition Upgrade | No | Yes |
| One month of GTA+ | Yes (eligible early purchases) | Yes |

## What's in the Ultimate Edition Upgrade

Rockstar says the upgrade is **integrated into Jason and Lucia's story progression**, so items unlock as you play rather than all dropping on day one. The announced contents include:

- **1995 Grotti Cheetah**
- **Hawk and Little Morgan revolver set**, plus personalized weapon variants
- **Vice City styles** for Jason and Lucia
- Jason's safehouse vehicles, including a **Dinka Enduro** and **Crest kayak**
- **Ganado** retro build options
- **Shitzu Squalo** boat
- **1967 Vapid Dominator Buggy** and **Paradise Garage**
- **Goodtime Gear**, **PTT Youngin$ Compound** content and a **Classic Car Collection** activity
- Business and property content including **Rideout Customs**, **Sara's Unisex Salon**, **Stock 305**, **Electric Fang Tattoo** and **One-Eyed Willie's Mod Shop**

## What both editions get

The **Vintage Vice City Pack**, available to eligible early purchasers of either edition, includes the **1955 Vapid Stanier Sedan** and garage, appearance items and an exclusive weapon pattern. Both also include a promotional month of **GTA+**.

**Important:** that GTA+ month **auto-renews unless you cancel it**, and it must be claimed by March 31, 2027. Set a reminder.

## Who should buy Ultimate

- You know you'll put 100+ hours into Leonida
- You love GTA's car culture and want extra rides and properties early
- You'd spend $20 on in-game content later anyway

## Who should buy Standard

- You mainly care about the story
- You're unsure how much you'll play past the campaign
- You'd rather see whether the extra content matters before paying for it

Because the upgrade content is spread through the story, it's mostly bonus convenience and flavor rather than something that changes the core game. Nothing Rockstar has announced suggests Standard owners get a lesser story.

## A note on physical copies

Rockstar's own store package is **code-in-box with no disc**. Retailer listings may differ, so check the specific product if a disc matters to you.

## The verdict

For most players, **Standard is enough**. The story, the world and the core game are identical. Ultimate is a good deal only if you already know you'll live in Leonida for months. One caveat: Rockstar hasn't said whether the Ultimate Edition Upgrade will be sold separately later, so if you want it, don't assume you can add it after launch.
