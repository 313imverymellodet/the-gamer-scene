If you're heading to a store for **Grand Theft Auto VI**, read this first: **the boxed version does not contain a game disc.** Rockstar's retail product is a [code in a box](https://kotaku.com/physical-copies-of-gta-6-will-just-be-a-code-in-a-box-with-no-disc-at-launch-2000709919), which you redeem on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store before downloading the full game.

And that download is big: [up to 212 GB](/news/2026-11-05-gta-6-preload-guide).

## What's in the box

According to [coverage of the retail package](https://www.gtaboom.com/the-gta-6-physical-copy-controversy-explained-3866) and [early unboxings](https://timesaver.gg/blog/gta-6-physical-copy):

- A download code for the game
- Packaging and a poster
- **No disc**

Only the **Standard Edition** is sold this way. There's no boxed Ultimate Edition, though reports say boxed buyers can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade through the PlayStation or Microsoft store after redeeming the code. The box costs the same [$79.99](https://www.gtaboom.com/the-gta-6-physical-copy-controversy-explained-3866) as the digital Standard Edition.

## Why would Rockstar do this?

Rockstar hasn't laid out a detailed public explanation, but reporting offers two clear motives.

**Leak prevention.** Discs tend to reach warehouses, shops and dataminers ahead of release. [Kotaku tied the decision to leak concerns](https://kotaku.com/physical-copies-of-gta-6-will-just-be-a-code-in-a-box-with-no-disc-at-launch-2000709919), which is understandable for a game hit by a [major 2022 hack](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-hacker-arion-kurtaj-guilty-plea) and this year's [CyberLeek leaks](/news/2026-08-20-gta-6-leak-crisis-cyberleek).

**Cost and control.** A code avoids pressing and shipping discs. It also removes the secondhand copy from the equation.

## Why people are upset

**Resale is gone.** Once a code has been redeemed, the box has little practical value. You can't trade it in or sell it on as a working copy.

**Lending is gone.** You can't hand a friend a box after you finish.

**Preservation.** A disc doesn't guarantee permanent access, especially for a game of this size, but a physical copy gives owners something independent of a storefront. Critics fear a code in a box is a digital license dressed up as a product.

**Internet access.** A 212 GB download is a serious burden for anybody with a slow or capped connection. New Zealand's Chorus [noted that it's more than a quarter of a typical household's monthly data](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/nz-and-aussie-gamers-among-first-to-play-gta-vi).

**The "physical" label.** Kotaku pointed out that this sits awkwardly with Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick's earlier dismissal of claims that physical copies would be delayed.

## Will a real disc ever come out?

Possibly. Kotaku's report indicated that a disc version would follow later, which would mean fans who want one could end up buying the game twice. No firm date has been widely confirmed, and Take-Two has [said such news will come from Rockstar](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114580/grand-theft-auto-6-pc-and-physical-disc-news-will-come-from-rockstar-confirms-take-two/index.html).

If you care about owning a disc, there's a case for holding off and watching for an announcement.

## Should you buy the box anyway?

**Reasons to buy it:** you want to pay cash, you're giving it as a gift, or you like having something on the shelf.

**Reasons to skip it:** you expected a disc, or you plan to resell. If the code is all you need, buying directly from your console's store saves you a trip.

**Before Black Friday:** check console bundles carefully. A bundle advertising GTA 6 almost certainly includes a code. Our [Black Friday guide](/news/2026-11-15-black-friday-2026-gaming-deals-guide) has more on what to look for.

## Beware of disc scams

Because there's no official disc at launch, any listing offering a real GTA 6 disc right now deserves heavy suspicion. So do "unused codes" from unknown sellers. Our [scam guide](/news/2026-11-15-gta-6-scams-how-to-spot) explains how to protect yourself.
