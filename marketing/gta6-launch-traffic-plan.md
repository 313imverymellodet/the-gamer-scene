## 4. Reddit and communities

Reddit is the biggest GTA audience, and also the strictest about self-promotion.
- **r/GTA6** and **r/GamingLeaksAndRumours**: read each sub's rules first. Most ban direct self-promo links. Comment helpfully in launch megathreads; don't drop links.
- **r/PS5 / r/XboxSeriesX**: the PS5-vs-Xbox and 60fps pieces answer questions people ask daily. Answer in the comment itself and link only if the sub allows it.
- Rule of thumb: 9 genuinely helpful comments for every 1 link.
